For site owners

Put our predictions
on your site

One line of code shows today's model predictions on your pages. It updates on its own, fits the space it finds, and carries your partner code on every click — so the signups it sends us are credited to you.

Install

<script src="https://www.betredge.com/widget.js" async
        data-ref="YOUR-CODE" data-sport="all" data-limit="3"
        data-lang="en" data-theme="auto"></script>

Paste it where you want the widget to appear — a sidebar, the middle of a post, a footer. That is the whole integration: no account, no API key, no build step. We send you the code that goes in data-ref.

How it works

01

You paste one tag

It loads after your content, in its own frame. It cannot block your page, and your CSS cannot break it — nor can it break your layout.

02

We keep it fed

The matches come from the same board our subscribers read, refreshed as our model publishes. You never edit the tag again.

03

You get the credit

Every link opens BetRedge with your code attached. We record which of your pages the visit came from, so performance is visible per site, not just per channel.

Two versions

Standard

The top match per sport is shown in full — teams, and the model's call with its confidence. The rest stay visible but their call is reserved for BetRedge. When the model has no clear favourite, the widget says so instead of inventing one.

Open

Every pick visible, nothing held back. Reserved for partners who send real traffic, and enabled by us on your code — there is nothing to change in the tag.

What it does not do

  • No cookies, no storage, no fingerprinting on your page. We do not identify your visitors, profile them, or pass anything to third parties.
  • No layout takeover. It takes the width you give it and sets its own height; it never opens overlays or navigates your page away.
  • No empty box. When no matches are scheduled it says so and keeps the link — it never invents a fixture.

We count how many times the widget was seen and clicked, and on which domain. That is all we store — no country, no visitor identifier. Like any remote asset (a font, an image, an embedded video), the browser must connect to our server to fetch it, so we see the IP that connection carries; we do not store it or derive anything from it. We believe this asks nothing new of your cookie policy, but you know your own setup — check it if in doubt.

Before you go live

  • Ask us for your partner code and put it in data-ref — without it the widget still works, but nothing can be credited to you.
  • Pick the sport and how many matches suit the space you have. Three is a good default in a sidebar; one works inline.
  • Check it on a phone: the widget is fluid and will follow your column.
  • Tell us if your audience sits in a country where this content should not run, and we will keep it off your site.
